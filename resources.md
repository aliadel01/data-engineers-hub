## Essential Books

### Fundamentals & Data Modeling

- **Data Warehouse Toolkit** by *Ralph Kimball & Margy Ross* — Core principles of dimensional modeling and star schema design.
- **Fundamentals of Data Engineering** by *Joe Reis & Matt Housley* — Overview of the data engineering lifecycle, architecture patterns, and tool evaluation.
- **Designing Data-Intensive Applications** by *Martin Kleppmann* — The definitive guide to storage engines, distributed consensus, transactions, and stream processing.

### Distributed Systems & Streaming
- **Streaming Systems** by *Tyler Akidau, Slava Chernyak, and Reuven Lax* — Deep dive into watermarks, windows, event time, and processing guarantees.
- **Grokking Streaming Systems** by *Jowanza Joseph* — A visual and practical introduction to real-time event processing concepts.
- **Apache Kafka: The Definitive Guide** by *Gwen Shapira, Todd Palino, Rajini Sivaram, and Krit Petty* — Architecture, internals, and operations for Kafka event streaming.
- **Learning Spark (2nd Edition)** by *Jules S. Damji, Brooke Wenig, Tathagata Das & Denny Lee* — A practical guide to Spark 3.x, covering Structured APIs, performance optimization, Adaptive Query Execution (AQE), and scalable streaming pipelines.
